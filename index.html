OggyLoop

Your loops. Your music. Live.
OggyLoop is an audio loop player designed for musicians and live performance. Import your own backing tracks, drum loops and audio files, organize them into sessions and trigger them instantly while you play.
Instant audio triggering
Loop and One Shot playback
Multiple pads
Tempo control and time-stretching
Drum-loop and backing-track workflow
Integrated PDF viewer for scores, lyrics and notes
OggyLoop does not include an audio-loop library. You use your own audio files and documents.
